Write, snap, keep writing.
The fastest way to take photo notes.

A camera floats over your note. Press the shutter and the photo lands right at the cursor.
Build blog-style notes with photos without ever stopping.

Read the guide GitHub

Features

A floating camera, always ready

A small camera window floats over the editor. Move it, resize it, minimize it, or go full screen.

Photos go where the cursor is

Shoot in the middle of a sentence and the text splits around the photo, so you can keep writing.

A real camera

Lens switching, zoom, tap to focus, exposure compensation, flash, timer, grid, HDR and manual controls.

Circles, arrows and text on photos

Draw circles, arrows, rectangles, freehand lines and text on your photos. Edit them again later.

Markdown files, Obsidian-ready

Notes are .md files and photos live in an attachments folder. Open the folder as an Obsidian vault.

No save button

Everything is saved as you type. No account, no ads, no network access.

Requirements

  • A smartphone with a camera running Android 9 or later
