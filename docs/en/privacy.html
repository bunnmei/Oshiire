Privacy policy

oshiire / Effective and last updated: October 17, 2026

This app collects no personal information. Your photos and notes are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer or any server.

This privacy policy explains how the Android app "oshiire" (the "App"), provided by bunnmei (the "Developer"), handles user information.

1. Information we collect

The Developer does not collect any information through the App.

  • There is no account or sign-in.
  • No name, email address, location, contacts or similar data is accessed.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other SDKs that send data elsewhere are used.
  • The App does not have the internet permission and never communicates over the network.

2. Photos and notes

  • Photos you take and notes you write are stored in the App's own folder on your device (Android/data/space.webkombinat.oshiire/files/vault).
  • No location is recorded in photos.
  • When you insert a photo from the gallery, only the photo you pick is copied into the App's folder. No other photos are accessed.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes your saved notes and photos from the device.
  • Files you copy to a PC or open in other apps are handled by that destination or app.

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
CameraTo take photos for your notes

Camera images are used on the device only for taking photos and showing the preview, and are never sent to the Developer or third parties.

4. Sharing with third parties

The Developer holds no user information and therefore shares none.

If you obtained the App from Google Play, Google handles download and related information under the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children, and collects no personal information from anyone regardless of age.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may change, for example when the App's features change. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via GitHub Issues.

The App's source code is available on GitHub.
