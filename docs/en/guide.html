oshiire / Android 9 or later

Tap "New note", write, and press the shutter on the floating camera. The photo lands at the cursor and you can keep writing. Saving is automatic.

1. Screens

Switch screens with the bottom navigation bar.

WhereScreen
Left: "Notes"Note list → tap to open the editor (and from there, the photo drawing screen)
Right: "Settings"Theme (system / light / dark), storage location, version

The bottom bar is hidden in the editor to give you more room. Use "←" at the top left, or the system back gesture, to return to the list.

2. Note list

  • Notes are sorted by last update, newest first.
  • Each row shows a thumbnail (the first photo in the note) on the left, and the title, the first lines and the date on the right. Notes without photos get a coloured icon.
  • Tap a note to open and edit it.
  • Tap "New note" at the bottom right to start a new one.
  • Swipe a note to the left to delete it. Tap "Undo" in the bar that appears to bring it back.

Notes without a title show their first line as the title.

3. Writing a note

  1. Enter a titleA new note starts in the title field. Press "Next" on the keyboard to move to the body. The title is optional.
  2. WriteWrite as in any notepad. Markdown (## heading, - list, …) works as-is.
  3. Press the shutterPress the shutter on the floating camera and the photo is inserted at the cursor. In the middle of a paragraph, the text splits around the photo.
  4. Keep writingThe cursor moves just below the photo, so you can carry on. Take as many photos as you like; they stay in the order you took them.

Photo menu

Tap a photo in the note to open this menu.

ItemWhat it does

Top bar buttons

ButtonWhat it does
CameraShows or hides the floating camera

⋮ → Delete noteDeletes this note (it is moved to the .trash folder)
There is no save button

The note is saved whenever you pause typing for half a second, and immediately when you leave the screen or close the app. A note you leave empty is not kept.

4. Floating camera

The small camera window at the bottom right of the editor. Take photos any time while you write.

The window keeps its position and size when you open another note. When the keyboard appears, the window moves to stay above it.

5. Full-screen camera

Go full screen when you want to take your time. Photos still go to the cursor in your note. Tap the thumbnail at the bottom left, "×" at the top left, or go back to return to the note.

FeatureDescription
FlashCycles off → auto → on → torch (always on)
TimerOff → 3 s → 10 s
GridShows 3×3 composition lines
HDRBetter results in high-contrast scenes (supported devices only)
Exposure compensationAdjust brightness with a slider
ZoomZoom buttons (0.5x, 1x, 2x … depending on the device) or pinch
LensPick ultra-wide, telephoto and other lenses directly ("auto" switches lenses with zoom)
Camera settingsManual focus, manual exposure (ISO, shutter speed), white balance, scene modes (night, portrait …)

Only the features your device's camera supports are shown, so the options vary between devices.

6. Drawing on photos

Tap a photo in the note → "Draw on photo".

ToolHow to use
CircleDrag diagonally over the area to circle
ArrowDrag from the tail to the tip
RectangleDrag diagonally
PenDraw freehand
TextTap where you want the text and type it
Select / moveTap a drawing to select it, drag to move. Delete with the trash button; tap a colour to recolour it
  • Choose from 6 colours (red, yellow, green, blue, white, black) and 3 widths (thin, medium, thick). Text size follows the width.
  • Undo and redo are in the top bar.
  • Tap "✓" at the top right to save. If you go back without saving, you are asked whether to discard your changes.
  • Open the photo again later to move or delete what you drew (the original photo is kept).

7. How notes are stored, and Obsidian

Notes are plain files, not a database. The folder is shown under Settings → Storage.

Android/data/space.webkombinat.oshiire/files/vault/
  2026-10-17_124831.md          ← one file per note
  attachments/
    IMG_20261017_124905.jpg     ← photo (with drawings, if any)
    IMG_20261017_124905.orig.jpg       ← original photo before drawing
    IMG_20261017_124905.jpg.annot.json ← the drawings (for re-editing)
  .trash/                       ← deleted notes

A note looks like this:

# Morning café

Went to the café by the station.

![](attachments/IMG_20261017_124905.jpg)

Beautiful latte art.

Opening in Obsidian

  1. Copy the vault folder to your PCConnect the phone over USB, choose "File transfer", and copy the folder above to your PC.
  2. In Obsidian, choose "Open folder as vault"Select the copied vault folder. Notes appear with their photos.

Markdown files you write on the PC and copy back appear in the app's list too (![[file.jpg]] embeds are supported).

Uninstalling the app deletes your notes

The folder belongs to the app, so its contents are removed when you uninstall. Copy important notes to your PC.

9. Troubleshooting

The camera shows nothing, or says camera access is needed

Tap "Allow" in the window. If you denied it before, go to the device's Settings → Apps → oshiire → Permissions → Camera.

"The camera was disconnected"

Another app may be using the camera. Close it, then minimize and restore the window, or reopen the note.

The camera window covers my text

Drag the window away or minimize it with "–". The camera button in the top bar hides it completely. There is extra space at the end of each note, so scroll down to write below the window.

Photos come out sideways or upside down

The photo's orientation follows how you hold the phone. Shooting with the phone lying flat can confuse it; tilt it up slightly.

I want a deleted note back

Right after deleting, tap "Undo" at the bottom of the list. After that, the .md file is still in the .trash folder; move it back into the vault folder.

Moving to a new phone

Copy the vault folder via a PC to the same location on the new phone.
